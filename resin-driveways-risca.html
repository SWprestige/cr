What we see in Risca

A lot of Risca is older terraced stock with newer estates above it on the hillside. The older frontages are often concrete laid decades ago, and some of it is sound enough to overlay; plenty of it is not. The newer estates usually have block paving that has started to sink at the edges. We will tell you which of those you have before we quote.
